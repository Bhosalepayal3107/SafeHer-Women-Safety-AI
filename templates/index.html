Legal Rights & Cyber Reporting

If you receive threatening, sexually explicit, or blackmail messages:

  • National Cyber Crime Portal: Call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in
  • Voyeurism & Image Blackmail: Punishable under Section 66E, 67A IT Act & IPC Section 354C.
  • Cyberstalking: Punishable under IPC Section 354D with up to 3–5 years imprisonment.
  • Women's Helpline (24/7): Call 1091 or NCW Helpline 7827170170.

PROTECT YOURSELF

Privacy & Safety 🔐

Simple ways to protect your personal information and digital privacy.

Personal Safety Reminders

  • Never share OTPs or passwords with unexpected callers.
  • Review the permissions granted to your apps.
  • Avoid posting your live location publicly.
  • Share journey details only with people you trust.
  • Keep evidence of threatening messages when safe.
  • Use strong, unique passwords for important accounts.

Important Information

Trusted contacts and incident records are stored in your browser's local storage. Other people using the same device or browser profile may be able to access them. Clearing browser data can delete these records.

SafeHer does not guarantee emergency response, automatically contact authorities, or continuously track your location.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

About SafeHer 💗

A women's safety awareness and support web application.

Our Purpose

SafeHer brings useful safety tools together in one interface to help users prepare, recognize possible digital threats, plan journeys, and record incidents.

Features

  • Personal safety dashboard
  • Emergency message preparation and location sharing
  • Rule-based digital threat screening
  • Safe journey planning
  • Local incident records
  • Privacy and safety reminders

Use Responsibly

SafeHer is a student safety-support project, not a replacement for emergency services. In India, call 112 if you need emergency assistance and it is safe to make the call.
